Team Pursuit/Breakaway Scenario Cycling Simulator with Evolutionary Computation (GA) Strategy Discovery Dataset: BSON-format export of MongoDB data collection.
Your ORCID iD: 0009-0002-1640-5726
Your ORCID record is https://orcid.org/0009-0002-1640-5726


This dataset contains the experimental settings and results for a research project exploring the use of applying an evolutionary algorithm search to two scenarios from the sport of competitive cycling:

 A team pursuit track cycling race where the finish time can be used as a measure of 'fitness'.
A 'breakaway' scenario occuring at the end of a (road) race where fitness is tied to staying away from the chasing peloton and (ideally) winning.

Above and beyond demonstrating the core use of the genetic algorithm search for team-specific solutions, further research was conducted into investigating the robustness of these solutions, and to the effects of domain-specific noise within the simulation behaviour on the discovery of strategies and their properties.

The data is exported from a MongoDB database, and is contained in that database's compact BSON format (though it may be mapped to the more human-readable JSON format if needed).

This dataset is designed to support the reproducibility of research that has been published in peer-reviewed environments, and therefore to support further efforts in this area. The application that runs the experiments, which includes the ability to show and search results and provide a step-by-step visualisation of solutions discovered from generation to generation, is available in its entirity as a GitHub-hosted project. Its URL is: https://github.com/aramicon/TeamPursuitModel

This research was conducted by Donal Kelly, under the supervision and support of Dr. Colm O'Riordan, both of the University of Galway, Ireland.
